Staff guide

Clock in and out in one tap

Your hours are recorded as you work them. No paper sheets, no texting your manager, no guessing at the end of the week.

How it works

Four steps, two taps

  1. Tap the tag at the cafe

    Hold your phone to the tag by the door. It opens a page for that location. No tag? Open My shifts and use the Clock in card.

  2. Sign in if asked

    You come straight back to the clock page afterwards.

  3. Press the big Clock in

    If you have a shift here, it is clocked against that shift. If not, it is recorded as time off the rota.

  4. When you finish, tap again and press Clock out

    The page only ever shows one button, so you can't press the wrong one.

Fixing a time

Got a time wrong? Three quick fixes

Just tapped by mistake

Undo

For 5 minutes after your last tap, press Undo. An accidental clock in is deleted. An accidental clock out reopens your shift. No manager needed.

Forgot to clock in or out

Use a different time

Choose Started at a different time? or Finished at a different time?, enter the time and a short reason. Your tap is still recorded, and your manager approves the time you gave.

Noticed it later

Ask to change it

On My shifts, your last 14 days are listed. Choose Ask to change, Ask to remove or Add a missing time.

Your original times stay as they are until a manager approves your request. You can see each request as waiting, approved or declined, and take it back while it is waiting.

FAQ

Questions

How do I correct a clock in or out reading?

Open My shifts and find the entry under Your clocked hours (last 14 days). Press Ask to change, enter the right time and a short reason (up to 200 characters), and send it. Your manager gets a notice and approves or declines it.

If it is an entry that should not exist, press Ask to remove. If you never clocked at all, press Add a missing time and give the location, date, start, finish and reason.

A few rules keep it fair: the time can't be in the future, one entry can't be longer than 24 hours, and it can't overlap another of your entries.

The app asked "Is 14:20 right?" when I clocked out

When you clock out more than 10 minutes after your shift ended, or well away from the cafe (200 metres by default), the app checks the time with you. Press Yes, clock out if it is right. If you actually finished earlier, press No, I finished at…, enter the time, and your manager approves it.

Do I need to wait for approval before I am paid?

A change to a time counts once a manager approves it. Until then your original times stand. Payroll shows clocked hours beside confirmed hours, so a later correction is always visible to whoever runs it.

Some cafes switch approval off. There, your changes apply straight away and are kept on record.

Does the app track where I am?

No tracking. When you clock in or out, your phone is asked once for its position. The cafe saves an approximate location (rounded to about 100 metres) so managers can tell you were at the cafe. Only managers can see it, it is deleted after 90 days, and it is described on the Privacy page.

If you say no to the location prompt, you can still clock in and out. Your manager may see that your position was not confirmed.

I am not on the rota but I am working

Clock in anyway. The page says "You're not on the rota here now" and records the time as off the rota at that location, so your manager can see it.

The tag page says I can't clock in

Clocking in and out has to be switched on for your cafe, and you need to be a member of it. Ask your manager or owner. If you work at several cafes, make sure the tag is for the cafe you are at.

What if I forget to clock out completely?

The system closes open entries automatically. You can then ask for the right finish time on My shifts, and your manager approves it.
