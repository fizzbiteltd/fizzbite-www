This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

Demo requests

When you send the demo request form we receive your name, business name, email address, venue type, any message you add, and the plan you were looking at. We use it only to reply to you and arrange a demo, and we keep it for as long as we are in contact about FizzBite.

Fonts

Clocking in and out

This section is for staff whose cafe uses FizzBite Rota to record working hours. The cafe you work for decides how clocking is used and is responsible for your records. FizzBite runs the system for them.

What is recorded

  • Your clock in and clock out times, the location you clocked at, and the shift they belong to.
  • Any change you ask for, with your reason, and whether a manager approved or declined it.
  • An approximate position from your phone, taken once when you clock in or out.

Your location

We do not track you. Your phone is asked for its position only at the moment you clock in or out. The position is rounded to about 100 metres before it is saved, so it shows whether you were at the cafe and nothing more precise.

  • Who can see it: only managers at your cafe.
  • How long it is kept: it is deleted after 90 days.
  • If you say no: you can still clock in and out. Your manager may see that your position was not confirmed.

Why it is used

Your times are used so you are paid for the hours you work and so the cafe knows who is on shift. Your position is used only to show that a clock in or out happened at the cafe.

To fix a wrong time, see the staff clocking guide.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the information held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to how it is used. For clocking records, start with your manager or the cafe owner, as they are responsible for them. You can also email us. If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
